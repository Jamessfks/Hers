Thirty voices, described in Google's own words. Picking one writes it into the file below, so you can still change it by hand.

Her face

Give her a photograph. It is what she looks like — the interface shows it, she sends it when you ask to see her, and every picture she generates of herself starts from it.

Her expressions

She can only show a face that exists. Each one is generated once from the photograph above, takes a few seconds, and costs about four pence — the same Gemini key, nothing else to sign up for. Once it exists she chooses when to use it, the way a face moves while talking.

What she remembers

Everything she has kept about you. Edit a line to change it, or cross it out. Changes apply the next time she wakes.

Setup

Gemini API key

This is the one thing she cannot work without. It is checked with Google before it is saved, then written to the .env file next to package.json — it stays on this machine and is never sent back to this page.

Get one at aistudio.google.com/apikey.

Reach her on Telegram

The same companion, on your phone — one memory, one mood. Make a bot with @BotFather, send it /newbot, and paste the token it gives you. It is checked with Telegram before it is saved, then written to .env and kept on this machine.

One step left, and only you can do it: open the chat and press Start. A bot is not allowed to message first, and nothing in Telegram tells it which chat is yours — so the first chat to speak is the one she is locked to, and it is written down for good.

Let her read your files

She can read the folders you tick, once, so she knows who she is talking to before you say anything. Only plain text is opened; everything else contributes its name. Anything that looks like a password, a key or a wallet is skipped without being opened. What she keeps is a handful of facts — the text itself is not stored and not kept in her context.

This does not make her feel closer to you. Knowing about someone is not the same as knowing them, and that is still earned.

How close she is

Earned, not set. It moves by days spent talking to her and nothing else — a stranger on the first day, and years of turning up before it means anything. You can put it wherever you like, and she will believe you.
