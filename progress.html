Anna

An always-on AI companion who lives at the edge of your screen: a full-body VRM character in a transparent, always-on-top macOS window, driven by your own API keys. Electron and three.js; nothing hosted, nothing leaves the machine except the provider calls you configured.

Turn loop core/orchestrator/companion.ts Done Overlapped synthesis with a two-clause lookahead cap, strictly ordered emission, immediate barge-in. The latency budget, clause ordering and the concurrency cap are asserted directly in companion.test.ts.

The 800ms budget

Past roughly a second, a reply stops reading as a person thinking and starts reading as a machine processing. Every structural decision in the codebase is downstream of this number. It only holds because the three stages overlap rather than queue.

0200ms400ms600ms800ms

  • 120ms Model time to first token
  • 180ms Enough tokens to make a first clause worth speaking
  • 90ms Cartesia time to first audio
  • 410ms Headroom, left for a bad network
  • 390ms Typical, against a budget of 800ms

Known gaps

Stated plainly, because a status page that implies otherwise wastes your time. This list is the README's own.

  • No settings UI. Keys and provider choice go through IPC handlers that work, but the window that calls them is not built. Configure by editing ~/Library/Application Support/Anna/config.json for now.
  • The video-avatar backends are a seam, not an implementation. heygen and tavus are valid values of AvatarRendererId with nothing behind them.
  • No sit or stand locomotion. sit_down holds a pose; she does not walk.
  • Ambient audio sensing is mic-triggered only. She does not listen to the room when you are not talking to her, by design, but that also means she cannot notice that you sighed.
  • macOS only. The window behaviour and every sensor is AppKit-specific.
